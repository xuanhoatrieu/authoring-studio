# Editorial conventions

Apply [natural-writing editing](natural-writing.md) to all book projects while
drafting and before delivering revised prose. It is shared guidance, not a tourism
profile option. Adapt to the book profile. The tourism profile targets adult readers including
farmers, household businesses, cooperatives and local support staff. Respect adult
experience; accessible writing does not mean a childish voice.

## Prose

- Scientific accuracy comes from definitions, evidence, reasoning and stated limits.
- Use familiar Vietnamese, concrete subjects and active verbs. Explain an unfamiliar
  term before using it in a diagram or procedure. Avoid unnecessary English.
- Develop one main idea per paragraph. Split layered sentences when clarity improves;
  do not impose English readability formulas or fixed Vietnamese word counts.
- Remove empty openings and inflated phrases such as “có thể nói rằng”, “vô cùng
  quan trọng”, “trong bối cảnh hiện nay” when they add no meaning. Replace vague
  “tăng cường/đẩy mạnh” with an action, actor and object where appropriate.
- Preserve uncertainty and conditions; do not strengthen “có thể” into a guarantee.
- Use prose for explanations, lists for genuinely parallel items or steps, tables
  for comparisons, and diagrams for processes and relationships.
- Remove unnecessary counts from headings, captions, alt text and labels inside
  images: prefer “Các câu hỏi mô tả trải nghiệm” to “Bốn câu hỏi để mô tả trải
  nghiệm”, and “Câu hỏi lựa chọn việc ưu tiên” to “Hai câu hỏi để chọn việc ưu
  tiên”. Retain counts that define a task or carry meaning, such as an exercise
  asking the reader to choose three priorities or numbered procedural steps.
- Explain an idea in the body once. Captions identify the visual; labels identify
  its parts. Do not repeat the paragraph in the image and again in its caption.
  Remove routine defensive phrases such as “ảnh không xác nhận…” when a precise
  description already avoids the unsupported claim. Keep a material limitation
  in the relevant explanation when it changes how the reader should understand
  or apply the content; do not merely hide it in working records.
- Test a qualification against the actual claim: retain limits that affect
  interpretation or use; remove defensive sentences about conclusions the passage
  never raises. A description of an activity need not add a disclaimer about
  unclaimed commercial results. Do not remove a limitation needed for a causal,
  quantitative or generalizing claim. See natural writing for contextual review.
- Read body prose, visible image text and caption together. Name figures directly
  using [caption rules](images-layout.md), and avoid repeating a hypothetical-case
  notice in all three places. Preserve the notice where readers need it.

## Examples and exercises

Integrate information → analysis → conditions of application without mechanically
printing those labels. With the agreed profile, use “Thông tin”, “Ví dụ”, or
“Tình huống” if a label is helpful. Do not write “dữ kiện thực tế”, “dữ liệu thực tế”,
“tình huống thực tế” or “hình ảnh thật” as labels in the book.
This wording rule must never conceal uncertainty or misrepresent generated material.

Actual cases require support. Distinguish what a business did from the author's
recommendation. Without outcome data, analyze the method without claiming revenue
or visitor growth. Clearly identify a hypothetical exercise as an assumption when
used, and do not assign invented results to real people or businesses.

Exercises specify the task, inputs, expected output and self-assessment criteria.
Chapter-end placement is the provisional tourism default; it was not explicitly
confirmed instead of section-end placement. Do not claim it was approved.
